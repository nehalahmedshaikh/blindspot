# Research note: who is visible in global development data?

## Preregistered question

Are recent reporting gaps across the selected SDG series associated with income group, region, population, and indicator family?

## Planned analysis

1. Freeze the first validated source snapshot and its checksum.
2. Describe completeness distributions by income group, region, and goal.
3. Fit a country-clustered descriptive model of cell-level presence using only predeclared covariates.
4. Report effect sizes, uncertainty, missing context variables, and sensitivity to modeled/estimated observations.
5. Repeat without population weighting and without conditional indicators.

## Interpretation boundary

The study is observational. Income group, region, and population are contextual correlates; they do not identify why a measurement is absent. The UN database is itself one reporting system and is not a census of every dataset held by national governments.

Results will be added after the initial snapshot is peer-checked. Publishing this protocol first reduces the temptation to invent a story after inspecting rankings.
