# Related work and novelty boundary

Blindspot does not claim that mapping data gaps is unprecedented.

- Open Data Watch's Open Data Inventory evaluates the coverage and openness of official national statistics.
- PARIS21's Statistical Capacity Monitor evaluates national statistical systems across planning, production, dissemination, use, and investment.
- The UN and World Bank publish data-availability measures and research on data deserts.
- The Sustainable Development Report visualizes outcomes and trends across the SDGs.

Blindspot's intended contribution is an open, continuously reproducible synthesis at country–series–year resolution: status-preserving coverage metrics, source snapshots and revision deltas, a challengeable priority score, temporal continuity evaluation, and downloadable evidence behind every public view.

The project should use “open measurement-gap observatory” or “distinctive synthesis,” not “world's first,” unless a formal systematic review later supports a narrower novelty claim.
