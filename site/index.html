04

Designed to be challenged

Every claim links back to a definition, source status, and downloadable row.

Absence ≠ failure

“Not observed” means the selected official source has no matching country–series–year observation. It does not prove a country failed to collect data.

Status is preserved

Country, adjusted, estimated, modeled, and global-monitoring observations remain distinguishable rather than being silently collapsed.

Applicability first

Conditional indicators are visible for exploration but excluded from priority scores until a defensible country-level applicability registry exists.

Prediction is experimental

The continuity model is evaluated through temporal backtests and yields to a simpler baseline when it does not improve calibration.
